## about

I started mentoring in technical education three years ago. Since then I have taught 6,000+ students online and run in-person cohort batches. At Moonpreneur I taught robotics, Python, machine learning basics and agentic AI to learners aged 6 to 20, and moved up to Level 3 in about eight months.

The cloud side comes from my engineering degree (B.Tech Hons, Lovely Professional University, CGPA 8.8) and an internship at MoEngage, where I worked with AWS, Azure and CI/CD pipelines. When I teach cloud, it is something I have actually used.

Alongside teaching I run KATS Guru, where we do training, software projects and marketing for clients.

## why one-to-one

A batch moves at the speed of the average student. A one-to-one lesson moves at yours.
