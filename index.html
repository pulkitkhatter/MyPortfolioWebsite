Meri baat

Three years of teaching, one way of doing it.

I started mentoring in technical education three years ago. Since then I've taught 6,000+ students online and run in-person cohort batches. At Moonpreneur I taught robotics, Python, machine learning basics and agentic AI to learners aged 6 to 20, and moved up to Level 3 in about eight months.

The cloud side comes from my engineering degree (B.Tech Hons, Lovely Professional University, CGPA 8.8) and an internship at MoEngage, where I worked with AWS, Azure and CI/CD pipelines. So when I teach cloud, it is something I have actually used.

Alongside teaching I run KATS Guru, where we do training, software projects and marketing for clients.

The thing I'm best at

Why one-to-one works

A batch moves at the speed of the average student. A one-to-one lesson moves at yours.

1

Built around you

Your goal sets the syllabus. A job switch, an exam, a school project, a certification. We start from there.

2

You set the speed

Stuck on one topic? We stay on it. Already know it? We skip ahead. Nobody is waiting for you.

3

You build things

Each topic ends with something you made: a pipeline, a script, a working robot.

4

Ask the silly question

There is no audience. Ask whatever you like, as many times as you need.
